Why the risk page is the product

Arizona rural dirt dies two ways and both are in the file, not on the map. Frontage to a mapped road is not a built road, and a certificate is on the land only, so anything built before foreclosure belongs to whoever redeems. The abstract names both on the parcel you are buying, with the feet and the tax year that make it checkable, plus the running total at the window and the statute for each step.
