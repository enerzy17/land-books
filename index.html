Why this is $99 and not free

The county list is public and useless. It runs 11,000 rows of legal descriptions, half of them landlocked, most of them over the number that makes an exit work. The book is the filter, the geometry run, the pricing arithmetic and the road and flood grade. Do it yourself and it is a day and a GIS licence. $99 buys the day back.

How the buy works

  1. Pay at the page. The book downloads in the same minute as a PDF.
  2. Pick a row. Phone the county treasurer and ask if the certificate is still unassigned. Stock moves, the list is a snapshot.
  3. Pay the all-in amount at the window. In Arizona the treasurer shall assign the certificate to anyone who pays, $10 fee, A.R.S. 42-18122. No auction, no bidder number.
  4. Assign it to a dealer in ten days, sell it for cash in thirty, or carry the note at 12% and sell the note after six payments.

Amounts reconcile to the cent against the county's own published list at generation. Arrears move with the tax roll, so re-pull the number the morning you drive.
