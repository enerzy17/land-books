What the columns mean

  1. All-in. Arrears plus county fees plus the $10 statutory assignment fee. The whole amount you hand over at the window.
  2. Frontage. Feet from the parcel to the nearest mapped road centerline, measured in the county GIS. Zero means the road touches it.
  3. Road. Maintained, mapped dirt, or platted only. Platted only is priced at 60% and sold as a camp parcel.
  4. Paper. The retail price on a carried note. 15% down, 12% simple, 60 months. Down and monthly printed, no credit check, the land is the collateral.
  5. Assign. What a local dealer pays for the certificate in ten days. All-in plus 60% of the spread, floored at twice the money.
