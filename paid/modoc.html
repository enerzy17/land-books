How redemption actually works

Phone the Modoc Tax Collector, 530 233 6223, give the APN, and ask for the current redemption figure. It moves with every roll, so the printed number is the starting point, not the final one. Pay it, the default comes off, and the parcel is clean of the tax debt. There is no certificate to hold and no assignment fee. If you would rather sell the situation than buy it, the flip column is what a bordering owner or a local handyman pays for a parcel at that price.

The auction, if you wait

Modoc sells defaulted parcels at public auction on Bid4Assets once the default is five years old, R&T 3691 and 3692. Every row here is somewhere on that path. Watch the ones on a road; opening minimums on old defaults are routinely under the parcel value.

Do this next

  1. Save the PDF. The link stays live, but save it.
  2. Pick your rows and phone the county treasurer the morning you buy. Ask whether the certificate is still unassigned. Stock moves and every list is a snapshot.
  3. Pay the all-in amount at the window, plus the $10 assignment fee if it is not already inside the figure. Arizona: A.R.S. 42-18122, the treasurer shall assign to anyone who pays.
  4. Work the exit you bought the row for: assign in ten days, cash in thirty, or carry the note at 12% and sell it after six payments.

If a number is wrong

Reply to the receipt. If an amount disagrees with the county's own published list, full refund the same day. Amounts reconcile to the cent at generation.
